System · Overview

A double-entry ledger, running

A ride-hailing marketplace's money: trips become zero-sum money orders, flow through Kafka into an append-only ledger, get collected by card, settled by the provider and paid out to drivers. Every figure here is read from the running services.

Payments · Reconciliation

Reconciliation

Each closed day, FakeCard's settlement report is matched line by line against our attempts. The settlement is booked as reported; every disagreement becomes a typed break and stays as a residual on the provider's clearing account. Nothing is adjusted away.

Payments · Payouts

Payouts

A payout run pays each eligible driver exactly what the ledger says they are owed, read at that moment, and refuses outright when the pipeline is more than 5 s stale. At most one payout per driver and currency is ever in flight; the database enforces it, not a check.
